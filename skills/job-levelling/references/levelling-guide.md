# Levelling guide (fictional client)

The definitions are in the job architecture (`job_architecture` tool). This guide says how to
weigh them.

1. **Scope first.** What does the role own: its own tasks (L1-L2), an area or small project (L3),
   several areas or a team (L4), a function or department (L5), a business unit or the company
   in one domain (L6)?
2. **Then autonomy.** Following procedures under supervision caps a role at L2. Setting
   priorities for an area is L4; setting direction and standards for a function is L5; setting
   strategy and answering to the board is L6.
3. **Impact confirms.** Own output (L1-L2), the team's results (L3), a department's results (L4),
   a function's business results (L5), company results and external representation (L6).
4. **People management** is evidence of scope, not a level by itself: leading a small team
   (up to about 8) fits L4; managing managers fits L5 or above. A senior expert with no reports
   can be L4 or L5.
5. **Years** asked for are weak evidence: use them only to break a tie between neighbouring
   levels, and never against clear scope and autonomy.
6. **Titles** are not evidence. "Senior", "Lead" and "Head" are used loosely in job adverts.
7. When the evidence spans two levels, propose the lower, give the higher as `alternativeLevel`,
   and set `needsReview`.

## Worked examples

- "Prepares monthly supplier invoices for posting, following the defined procedure; checks with
  the team lead when a case is unusual. 1 year of experience." → finance L1: own tasks, procedures,
  escalates exceptions.
- "Plans the maintenance programme of one depot's vehicles independently and coordinates the
  workshop; guides two apprentices. 4 years of experience." → operations L3: a complete area,
  plans own work, informal guidance.
- "Leads a team of 6 backend engineers, sets priorities for the payments area and coaches
  others." → engineering L4: a team, sets priorities for an area.
- "Head of Fleet: manages the depot managers of all depots, sets the fleet strategy and is
  accountable for a budget of EUR 30 million." → operations L5 (a function; manages managers),
  L6 only if it also answers to the board for a business unit.
