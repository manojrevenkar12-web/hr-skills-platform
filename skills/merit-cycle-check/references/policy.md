# Merit guideline 2026 (fictional client policy)

The full matrix is `data/reference/merit-policy-2026.yaml`; this page explains it.

- **Guideline range**: each proposal has a range of % increases by the employee's last rating
  (1-5) and where their full-time base pay sits in the salary band (compa-ratio = base / band
  midpoint: below 0.90, 0.90-1.00, 1.00-1.10, above 1.10). Lower in the band means a higher
  range, so pay converges on the midpoint over time.
- **Outside the range**: above the range needs a written reason (blocking without one, a
  warning with one); below the range is a warning to confirm.
- **Rating 1**: no increase.
- **Band maximum**: no proposal may take full-time base pay above the band maximum (120% of the
  midpoint); a promotion is a separate process.
- **Budget**: each org unit's proposals may cost at most 3.5% of its base payroll.
- **Equity**: within an org unit, women's and men's proposals are compared only within the same
  level and rating, weighted by the smaller group of each comparison. If women's average is 1.0
  point or more below men's, the unit's proposals are reviewed before sign-off. This is a check
  of a pattern, not a finding about any person.

The check advises the compensation partner and the manager. It approves and rejects nothing.
