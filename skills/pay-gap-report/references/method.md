# How the figures are defined

Source: Directive (EU) 2023/970 (Pay Transparency), Articles 3, 9 and 10. Germany has not yet
transposed it (October 2026); its expert commission's recommendations are noted where used.

| Indicator (Art. 9(1)) | Definition used here |
|---|---|
| (a) gender pay gap | (men's mean - women's mean) / men's mean × 100, on gross hourly pay |
| (b) gap in complementary or variable components | the same on annual complementary + variable pay per worker |
| (c) median gender pay gap | as (a) on medians |
| (d) median gap in complementary or variable components | as (b) on medians |
| (e) proportion receiving complementary or variable components | share of women, and of men, with any |
| (f) proportion in each quartile pay band | women and men ranked by hourly pay, split into four equal bands |
| (g) gap by categories of workers | (a) for basic pay and for total pay within each category |

- **Pay** is what was actually received in the year: base, complementary (allowances in cash or
  kind) and variable (bonus, commission). Hourly pay divides it by hours paid, so part-time work
  does not create a gap by itself.
- **Categories of workers** should group work of equal value (skills, effort, responsibility,
  working conditions; Art. 4). Here they are job family × level from the job architecture: a
  proxy that the client's job evaluation must confirm.
- **People recorded as "divers"** are counted, but the Directive's gap compares women and men.
- **Small groups**: a category with fewer than six women or six men shows counts only (German
  expert commission, November 2025).
- **Findings**: a category with a gap of at least 5% either way (Art. 10(1)(a)). It must be
  justified by objective, gender-neutral criteria or corrected within six months; otherwise a
  joint pay assessment with worker representatives follows.
- **Adjusted gap**: for a flagged category with at least 12 people, the gap in basic hourly pay
  after allowing for tenure and location (log-linear least squares). It shows how much of a gap
  these factors account for. It does not decide whether they are a valid justification.
